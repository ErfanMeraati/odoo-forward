Discuss Message Forward

Forward messages between Discuss conversations in one click — no copy, no paste, no lost context.

A lightweight extension for Odoo Discuss that lets your team select any message and instantly forward it to another channel or direct conversation.

HOW IT WORKS
Three steps. That's it.
01

Select Message

Pick the message you want to share from any conversation.

02

Choose Conversation

Select the channel or direct message to forward it to.

03

Forward Message

Confirm and it instantly appears in the destination.
